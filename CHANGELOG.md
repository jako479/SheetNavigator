# Changelog

What changed for users, grouped by release version. Not a commit log.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-30

- Initial release.
- Worksheets pane on the View tab lists the active workbook's visible sheets; click to jump.
- Pane open state, docked side and width are remembered per workbook file.
- A new pane opens on the side and at the width last used; right and 150 points to start.
- Works in every workbook window.
